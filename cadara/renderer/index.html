Legal & Help

Cadara desktop application

What Cadara does

Cadara turns natural-language specifications into CAD proposals using the AI provider and model you choose. Geometry is generated locally by the CAD runtime when that runtime is installed.

Privacy and data handling

API keys are stored locally using your operating system's secure storage when available. Prompts and reference images are sent to the selected AI provider to complete a request. Previous designs are stored locally in the app data directory. Review your provider's privacy, retention, and cross-border transfer terms before sharing confidential designs or personal data.

Generated CAD responsibility

AI output can be incomplete, inaccurate, or unsuitable for manufacture. Check dimensions, units, tolerances, materials, clearances, safety factors, intellectual-property rights, and applicable standards with a qualified professional before production or safety-critical use.

India and other jurisdictions

This notice is a product baseline, not legal advice. If Cadara is offered in India or processes personal data, publish a complete privacy notice and provide an appropriate contact or grievance route.
